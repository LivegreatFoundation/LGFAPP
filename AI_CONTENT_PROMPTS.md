## 🎯 Master Prompt Template

```
You are a content writer for the Live Great Foundation, a non-profit organization in Kenya focused on community development, women empowerment, education, health, and environmental sustainability.

Create [NUMBER] blog posts with the following specifications:

**Organization Context:**
- Location: Kenya, East Africa
- Focus Areas: Community development, women empowerment, education, health, environmental sustainability
- Tone: Inspiring, positive, impact-focused, authentic
- Audience: Donors, community members, partners, beneficiaries

**Content Requirements:**
1. **Title**: Engaging, 50-80 characters, SEO-friendly
2. **Content**: 500-800 words with proper HTML formatting
3. **Author**: Realistic Kenyan name (mix of male/female names)
4. **Category**: Choose from the provided list
5. **Tags**: 3-6 relevant, comma-separated tags

**HTML Formatting Required:**
- Use <h2> for main sections, <h3> for subsections
- Structure content with <p> tags for paragraphs
- Include <ul> and <li> for lists
- Add <blockquote> for testimonials/quotes
- Use <strong> for emphasis, <em> for notes

**Categories (choose one per post):**
- Health & Wellness
- Education  
- Community Development
- Women Empowerment
- Environmental Sustainability
- Youth Programs
- Success Stories
- Events & News
- Partnerships
- Fundraising

**Content Guidelines:**
- Include specific outcomes, statistics, or impact numbers
- Feature authentic testimonials or quotes
- Reference Kenyan locations, culture, or context
- Focus on real community transformation
- Maintain hopeful, empowering tone
- Include call-to-action or next steps

**Output Format:**
Present each blog post as a structured entry that can be easily copied into Excel:

Post 1:
Title: [TITLE]
Content: [HTML CONTENT]
Author: [AUTHOR NAME]
Category: [CATEGORY]
Tags: [tag1, tag2, tag3, tag4]

[Continue for all requested posts]
```

## 💡 Pro Tips for AI Content Generation

### Best Practices:
1. **Start Specific**: Use detailed prompts rather than generic requests
2. **Iterate and Refine**: Generate content, then use enhancement prompts
3. **Batch Process**: Create multiple posts at once for consistency
4. **Verify Facts**: Always fact-check AI-generated statistics and claims
5. **Maintain Voice**: Use follow-up prompts to ensure consistent tone
6. **Test Import**: Try importing 2-3 posts first to verify formatting

### Common AI Limitations:
- May generate unrealistic statistics
- Could miss cultural nuances
- Might create repetitive content structures
- May not understand local context fully
- Could produce overly optimistic outcomes

### Mitigation Strategies:
- Provide specific context in prompts
- Use fact-checking prompts for verification
- Request multiple variations for diversity
- Include authentic details in follow-up prompts
- Review and edit before importing
